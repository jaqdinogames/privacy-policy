JaqDino Games – Privacy & Support

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Data Collection

Our Stickerbook and Stickerland apps do not collect, store, sell, or share personal or device data. The apps do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Children’s Privacy

The apps are designed for children. They do not request names, email addresses, dates of birth, precise location, or any other personal information from children or adults.

Purchases and Network Access

Some of our apps include optional one-time in-app purchases that permanently unlock additional content. Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store payment details. An app may connect to Apple’s services to show products, complete purchases, and restore previous purchases. Its main game content can be used offline.

Advertising and Accounts

There are no advertisements, no subscriptions, and no user accounts.

Local Storage and Permissions

Game progress, settings, and creations may be stored locally on the device. We cannot access this locally stored information. The apps do not access contacts, location, the camera, or the microphone.
