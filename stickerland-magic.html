Stickerland Girls Games – Privacy & Support

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Data Collection

Stickerland Girls Games does not collect, store, sell, or share personal or device data. The app does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for children. It does not request names, email addresses, dates of birth, precise location, or any other personal information from children or adults.

Purchases and Network Access

The app includes optional one-time in-app purchases that permanently unlock additional worlds. Purchases and purchase restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store payment details. The app may connect to Apple’s services to show products, complete purchases, and restore previous purchases.

Advertising and Accounts

There are no advertisements, no subscriptions, and no user accounts.

Local Storage and Permissions

Game progress and creations are stored locally on the device. The app does not access contacts, location, the camera, or the microphone. Artwork is saved only inside the app after the user chooses the save function.
